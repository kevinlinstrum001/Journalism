# Stories

## Purpose

This directory contains living Stories for the Las Vegas, New Mexico environment.

A **Story** is an evolving analytical or narrative synthesis that draws from the Body. It is not limited to a publishable news article. A Story may persist for months or years as events develop, evidence accumulates, and interpretations change.

## A Story may track

- framing question or central problem;
- current synthesis;
- latest development;
- timeline;
- key entities;
- linked Subjects and Dossiers;
- supporting Sources and Investigations;
- open questions;
- research requirements;
- competing explanations;
- commentary and interpretation;
- last substantive update.

## Research-required state

A Story may explicitly enter a **research required** state when analysis reveals missing information. That gap should generate or link to an Investigation or other Assignment rather than being filled by assumption.

## Operating rule

> **Stories consume and interpret the Body; they do not replace the records that support them.**
