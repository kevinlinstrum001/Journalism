# Sources

## Purpose

This directory contains durable source-oriented records for the Las Vegas, New Mexico environment.

A **Source** may be a continuing information provider or a particular source artifact. These are related but distinct concepts.

Examples of continuing sources:

- government agencies;
- newspapers and broadcasters;
- podcasts and YouTube channels;
- institutional websites;
- archives;
- data portals;
- recurring publications;
- knowledgeable people or offices when formally used as sources.

Examples of source artifacts:

- one article;
- one podcast episode;
- one video;
- one public notice;
- one dataset release;
- one meeting agenda;
- one map;
- one report;
- one interview record.

## Source records may track

- identity and type;
- publisher or producing entity;
- direct route or URL;
- Subjects commonly covered;
- provenance;
- publication or access date;
- authority relevant to particular facts;
- known limitations;
- prior use in Dossiers, Stories, and Investigations;
- notes about methodology, framing, corrections, or recurring strengths and weaknesses.

Avoid reducing source evaluation to a single trust score. Preserve the evidence for any assessment.
