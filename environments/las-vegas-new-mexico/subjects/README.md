# Subjects

## Purpose

This directory contains Subject records for the Las Vegas, New Mexico environment.

A **Subject** is a durable organizational lens over the Body. It gives structure to an area of knowledge, points to relevant Body records, and can expose activity state, gaps, open questions, and relationships.

A Subject is not intended to duplicate every document relevant to it.

## Subject records should help answer

- What is this Subject about?
- What is in scope and out of scope?
- Which Dossiers, Stories, Investigations, Sources, entities, maps, datasets, and other Body records are relevant?
- What substructure is useful for understanding the Subject?
- When was the Subject first and most recently active?
- What important gaps or unresolved questions remain?

## Operating rule

> **Subject = structure and routing over the Body, not a private copy of the Body.**

A single Body record may support multiple Subjects. Preserve references rather than unnecessary duplication.
