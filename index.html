Systems Model & Vocabulary

A disposable working surface for developing a language capable of describing Las Vegas, New Mexico: what exists, what happens, how entities relate, how resources move, how information travels, and how conditions change.

Working rule: nothing on this page is precious. Terms may be renamed, split, merged, contradicted, or discarded as investigations expose better distinctions.

Basic grammar

The current experiment treats the town as a model that can be described with a small set of recurring concepts.

ENTITY → VERB / RELATIONSHIP → ENTITY ENTITY + ACTION + CONTEXT → OCCURRENCE STATE(before) → OCCURRENCE → STATE(after) RESOURCES → ACTOR / PROCESS → OUTPUT / SERVICE / STATE EFFECT SOURCE ARTIFACT → INVESTIGATION → FINDINGS → SYSTEM UPDATES

Entities

Entity is the broad working term for something with enough independent identity to track. Entity does not imply singularity.

Entity type

Person

An individual human. Roles such as mayor, judge, plaintiff, employee, or owner are relationships or contextual roles rather than permanent person types.

Entity type

Group

A collective, formal or informal, that may be tracked because it has identity, association, legal status, collective agency, or analytical significance.

Entity type

Building / Facility

A constructed physical asset whose identity is independent of its occupant, owner, and address.

Location concept

Address

A navigational/location identifier. It may be a field-work destination and should not be collapsed into the building itself.

Entity type

Place / Geographic Unit

A plaza, parcel, neighborhood, precinct, jurisdiction, district, corridor, site, or other independently useful geographic area.

Artifact / instrument

Governing Instrument

A law, ordinance, charter, regulation, contract, policy, resolution, or similar durable artifact that can establish authority, obligations, or organizations.

Groups and collective agency

Groups can arise through different mechanisms. The model should not assume that every useful grouping is an organization.

Working typeBasisExample
Formal group / organizationFormalized identity, rules, charter, incorporation, appointment, statute, or other recognized formation process.Corporation, agency, board, council.
Informal social formationShared identity, position, interest, coordination, or collective action.Faction, coalition, community of interest.
Legal / procedural groupingCommon legally recognized relationship to a proceeding or matter.Plaintiffs in a case.
Derived / relational groupComputed because members share a relationship to some entity, project, budget, place, or criterion.All contractors paid from a particular program.
Analyst-defined groupCreated for research because members share a relevant characteristic.Businesses within one precinct.

Possible dimensions to keep separate: collective agency, legal agency, self-identification, formalization, membership, and analyst-defined grouping.

Occurrences, states, and change

Working term

Occurrence

Something that happened. It may contain one or many actions and does not require a measurable state effect in something we currently track.

Working term

State

What is true about something at a particular time.

Working term

State Change

A before/after difference that can be observed or measured. An occurrence can cause zero, one, or many tracked state changes.

Working term

Relationship

A typed connection between entities, often expressible as a verb: owns, occupies, operates, represents, funds, employs, serves, regulates.

USPS → removes → Mailbox 42 Occurrence: mailbox removal Before state: Mailbox 42 present at 7th & Mills After state: Mailbox 42 absent from 7th & Mills If the mailbox inventory did not yet exist, the removal occurrence could still be recorded.

Resources and an economic lens

Economics is treated as a powerful interpretive vocabulary over the shared reality model, not as the only way to interpret the town.

CONDITION / NEED ↓ SERVICE REQUIREMENT ↓ DEMAND / CAPACITY GAP ↓ RESOURCE ALLOCATION ↓ PRODUCTION / TRANSFORMATION ↓ OUTPUT: ASSET / SERVICE / CHANGED CONDITION ↓ STATE EFFECT

scarcitydemandcapacityallocationinputoutputlaborcapitalproductionserviceexpenditureappropriationobligationpaymentstockflowrecurrence

A city may address a service requirement through existing employees and assets, by purchasing capacity, by contracting for a service, by changing service levels, or by leaving some need unmet. Money is one resource among labor, equipment, materials, information, time, land, energy, expertise, and administrative capacity.

Information and communication

Communication can itself be modeled as action. The same source artifact may provide evidence about its subject and about how information moves through the community.

Entity A → produces → Flyer Entity B → distributes → Flyer Person C → receives → Flyer Investigation → analyzes → Flyer Entity → communicates → content ↓ channel ↓ audience

Official notices, newspapers, websites, social media, flyers, conversations, arguments, meetings, witness observations, interviews, and secondary transmission can all be relevant when an investigation concerns information flow.

Investigation as interpretation

A source artifact does not directly become accepted knowledge. A bounded investigation can interpret it, distinguish claims from supported findings, and propose or make controlled changes to the system.

SOURCE ARTIFACT ↓ INVESTIGATION identify entities identify actions / verbs identify claims identify resources identify relationships test state changes preserve uncertainty ↓ INVESTIGATION SUMMARY ↓ SYSTEM UPDATES ↓ DOWNSTREAM NARRATIVE / SYSTEMS SYNTHESIS

Open questions

This page is intentionally unfinished. Some questions already exposed by the model:

What counts as an entity?

When should a recurring activity, project, legal instrument, service, asset, or social formation receive independent identity?

When is a group an organization?

What minimum evidence establishes formalization, collective agency, legal agency, or continuity?

What vocabulary is domain-specific?

Which terms belong to economics, government, law, anthropology, communications, operations, or another interpretive lens rather than the neutral core?

How should time work?

Relationships, states, roles, contracts, budgets, occupations, and memberships can all begin and end.

How much resolution is useful?

Provenance and relationships can be tracked at very fine granularity, but only when the research question makes that resolution useful.

What survives repeated investigations?

Recurring concepts should eventually become stable vocabulary and, only later, machine-readable schemas.
